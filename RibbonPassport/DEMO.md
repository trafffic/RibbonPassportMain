# Demo Pokémon

Tap **+ → Add / refresh demo team** after updating. These fictional example records demonstrate journeys that could produce their displayed awards. They are not imported saves, event distributions or legality-certified Pokémon files.

| Pokémon | Origin → current game | Features demonstrated |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sparky, shiny Pikachu | Sword → Sword | Nickname, female setting, shiny Gigantamax sprite (Max Soup), Galar Champion and Master Rank ribbons. No Gen 3 ribbons or transferred wild marks. |
| Lugia | XD → Alpha Sapphire | The existing Shadow Lugia artwork for its XD origin, National Ribbon from purification, Earth Ribbon from Mt. Battle, GameCube icon. The artwork commemorates its origin; the transferred Pokémon would be purified. |
| Shiny Rayquaza | Ruby → Alpha Sapphire | A Ruby/Sinnoh contest journey earning all 40 legacy contest ribbons, displayed as one gold Contest Memory Ribbon; Earth Ribbon after a GameCube visit, Artist Ribbon and regional championships. No restricted Battle Tower ribbons. |
| Metagross | Diamond → Scarlet | DS origin icon, six Gen 4 Battle Tower ribbons and 20 Sinnoh contest ribbons, displayed as two silver Memory Ribbons; later-generation championships and battle facilities along a Diamond → Platinum → Black/White → Bank → Gen 6 → Gen 7 → HOME → Sword/Shield → Scarlet journey. Metagross is available in Scarlet with The Indigo Disk. |
| Shiny Gardevoir | Alpha Sapphire → Alpha Sapphire | All five Master Contest ribbons plus Contest Star, Super Training, friendship, effort and Hoenn championship. A fuller contest board without later-generation awards. |
| Shiny Sylveon | Violet → Violet | A shiny wild Eevee with a Rare Mark evolved into Sylveon, later Partner/Gourmand/Itemfinder marks, Rare Mark title and a combined total of eight awards. Top-three ribbon displays still rank its ribbons rather than its marks. |

Awards are stored in their original form and consolidated for display by the current game's generation. A Pokémon cannot travel backwards from a later generation to earn an earlier-generation ribbon. The demo data follows that rule and avoids gift-only awards on ordinary caught Pokémon.

The Showdown widget gallery has its own illustrative battle-build preview. It doesn't insert those builds into your collection or claim they form a legal team for one competitive ruleset.
