# Ribbon Passport logo workshop

17 October 2026. Three preview concepts created with the built-in imagegen tool. Concept 4, Adventure Star, was subsequently selected as the release app icon; see `04-prompt.md`. Concepts 1–3 remain workshop alternatives. No reference artwork was supplied to these generations; the prompts requested original generic star awards rather than a reproduction of the in-game Contest Star Ribbon. These are design explorations, not trademark clearance.

1. `01-gold-star.png`: gold medallion with violet tails.
2. `02-violet-jewel.png`: gold star on a violet jewel, teal tails.
3. `03-simple-star.png`: simpler star with lavender inset and violet tails.

## Exact generation prompts

### Concept 1

Use case: logo-brand. Create one square 1024x1024 app icon concept for Ribbon Passport, a personal achievement journal. Original pixel art award ribbon: a large five-point gold star mounted on a small stepped gold circular medallion, two violet ribbon tails fanning below. Dark plum full bleed square background, no rounded outer mask. Crisp deliberate chunky pixel grid, limited gold, cream and violet palette, restrained pixel shading, no smooth gradients, no blur, no tiny details. Centered, generous margins, recognisable at 48 pixels. Evokes winning a contest, independently designed generic award. No characters, no ball emblems, no letters, no text. One icon only.

### Concept 2

Use case: logo-brand. Single square app icon concept for Ribbon Passport, a personal achievement journal. Independently designed chunky pixel art contest award: a small pale-gold five point star set in a rich violet diamond-shaped jewel medallion, a thin stepped gold rim, two short teal swallowtail ribbons below, tiny coral square accent at the top. Flat dark midnight-plum full bleed square backdrop, generous clear margin. 48x48 sprite aesthetic enlarged with crisp square pixels, limited 10-colour palette, very simple silhouette, restrained two-tone highlights, no smooth gradients or blur. Clearly a beautiful celebratory contest ribbon, not a military medal. No text, letters, characters, ball emblems, or official franchise symbols. One icon only.

### Concept 3

Use case: logo-brand. One 1024x1024 square app icon concept for Ribbon Passport. Original extremely simple chunky pixel art award ribbon, a large flat pale-gold five point star with one small lavender diamond inset, a compact lavender ribbon knot immediately behind the star and TWO SHORT violet ribbon tails underneath. No circular medallion, no scalloped rosette ring. Restrained 32x32 sprite aesthetic scaled up with square stepped edges and a consistent coarse pixel grid, only 7 solid colours, two-tone shading, no fine outlines, no gradients, no blurry edges, no texture. Warm midnight plum background reaching every square edge, no outer rounded mask. Centered compact bold silhouette, generous 18 percent margin, legible as a small home-screen icon. Cheerful contest achievement theme, independently designed generic award. No text, letters, characters, Pokeballs or other franchise symbols. One icon only.
